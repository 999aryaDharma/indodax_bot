## Files and contracts (owned paths only)

- `src/indodax_lab/portfolio/adoption.py` (CREATE) —
  - `AssetAssignment`: Strategy assignment for existing account assets, enforcing normalized identifiers, positive finite quantity, and strict cost basis invariants (if `CostBasisValidity.UNKNOWN`, `cost_basis` and evidence must be `None`; if `KNOWN`, requires positive finite value and evidence).
  - `AdoptedAsset`: Immutable adopted asset record bound to active strategy, retaining cost basis validity, evidence, and operational adoption baseline price/notional.
  - `UnadoptedAsset`: Inventory of account assets not adopted by strategies; contributes to account exposure but remains unmanaged.
  - `AdoptionProposal`: Generated by `propose_adoption` bound to snapshot revision, snapshot server time, reconciliation report/evidence, adopted assets, unadopted assets, and total account exposure.
  - `AdoptionRecord`: Durable approval record created by `approve_adoption` binding actor, reason, request ID, revision, release/owner references, quantity, cost basis validity, and reconciliation evidence.
  - `VerifiedDepositRecord`: Audited external cash flow deposit record without affecting trading PnL.
  - `ReviewedReconciliationRecord`: Explicit operator acknowledgement and resolution of reconciliation discrepancies.
  - `AdoptionStore`: Durable SQLite store (WAL mode, synchronous=FULL) with optimistic revision fencing and request ID/proposal ID idempotency.
  - `propose_adoption(...)`: Enforces PM-07-AC0 (rejects stale snapshots and unresolved venue orders), checks balance availability, prevents duplicate pair assignments, and inventories unadopted assets.
  - `approve_adoption(...)`: Enforces idempotency (duplicate request returns existing record without double-application), revision fencing, and commits position ownership.
  - `validate_strategy_sell(...)` / `can_strategy_sell(...)`: Enforces PM-07-AC2 (unadopted assets cannot be sold by any strategy; only adopting strategy can sell up to adopted quantity).
  - `is_new_entry_allowed(...)`: Enforces PM-07-AC3 (unexplained reconciliation discrepancies block new entry until explicitly reviewed and acknowledged via `review_reconciliation`).
  - `compute_portfolio_exposure(...)`: Verifies unadopted assets affect total exposure and marked equity.
  - `record_verified_deposit(...)`: Enforces PM-07-AC5 (deposits update cash and baseline, but do not appear as trading profit).
